# Generating a card on the fly

Living note. Decision [0016](../decisions/0016-broad-base-and-on-the-fly-generation.md)
recorded that **the application generates a card when you arrive at an
artist that has none**; it left open *when* that fires
([first-run.md](first-run.md) § To settle, point 3). This note holds the
subject.

## What set it off

On the morning of 2026-09-09, Joel: "I feel like listening to Jacques Brel
but he is not in the catalog. I could find him through the Spotify search,
but I cannot play the track and it does not create the artist card."

The behavior matched the code: from home, a Spotify result with no card
answers "nothing to branch from"; from listening, `:search` plays it as
`Offmap` but writes nothing. The catalog could only grow through
`tools/generate-cards.py`, outside a session.

A second observation, the same morning: Brel's card, once generated by the
script, carried three `similar` links towards Brassens, Moustaki and
Gainsbourg. Two had a card, the third did not — and `graph_neighbors`
(`engine.rs:179`) **throws away a link whose card is missing**. Half of
Brel's neighborhood was therefore invisible. It is the same gap seen from
the other end: the catalog does not grow along its own links.

## Decided

**Both triggers** (Joel, 2026-09-09).

1. **The search brings somebody new in.** Enter on a result outside the
   catalog generates its artist's card, commits it, then sets off from
   them. That is the morning's gesture, with no key to learn.
   `:generate <name>` does the same without going through a track;
   `:generate <name> <mbid>` when the search by name fails and the
   identifier was found by hand (Joel, 2026-09-09, "Oai Star"). In that
   case, if MusicBrainz does not answer at all, the card is born minimal —
   name, id, Deezer tops — rather than not at all.
   Two safeguards since the same day: **MusicBrainz is often busy** (503 for
   a minute), so we wait up to half a minute and say "busy, try again"
   rather than "not found"; and **a name coming from a slug has lost its
   apostrophes** ("Lojo" for Lo’Jo), which the MusicBrainz search does not
   recover — Deezer does, and lends the spelling, verified against the same
   key as the cards.
2. **Arriving grows the catalog along its links.** A link towards a missing
   card stops being thrown away: it shows up as a gap branch, and taking it
   generates the card before walking.

Neither one is enough alone: the first can only bring in what you name, the
second only what the catalog already points at.

**A gap can also be generated without being taken** (Joel, 2026-09-19/20,
[before-release.md](before-release.md) workstream C): `fg<n>` brings the
card into being, the gap row becomes a branch on screen, and the gaps
refresh around the fresh card. **A gap's branch is a walk** like any
proposed branch (`engine::branch_from`), not an encore of the generated
artist.

**It is an edit in the sense of [0013](../decisions/0013-keyboard-tuning-measure-or-edit.md)** —
a readable commit, `generated = true` until reviewed — as 0016 intended.

**The catalog becomes mutable during a session.** It was lent read-only for
the whole duration of a listening session (`Live<'a> { catalog: &'a
Catalog }`), so a card written only existed at the next launch — which the
key table accepts for edits ("an edit only counts for the engine at the next
launch"). For a generation that is untenable: you ask for it in order to
listen *now*. So the session now owns its catalog and inserts the card into
it when the background work returns.

**The vector is born with the card**
([0019](../decisions/0019-the-application-vectorizes.md), Joel, 2026-09-09,
after the measured trial below). The application vectorizes itself with
`fastembed`: same model and same composed text as the Python script,
truncation at 128 tokens, normalized vectors, in the **same commit** as the
card; `forkstify vectors` regenerates the index and replaces
`tools/vectoriser.py`. The trial: cosine ≥ 0.999999 over the 316 cards, cold
build 26 s, +35 MB, `g++` in the image, a 241 MB model on first use; the
Python index was not normalized, and now is.

## Direction

**The sources are the script's**, already proven over 316 cards:
MusicBrainz for identity, dates, origin, genres and typed relations; Deezer,
with no key, for the tops and the similars. The pipeline is the one in
[catalog.md](catalog.md) § The cold start — "one pipeline, three moments",
and listening is the third.

**One difference from the script, and only one**: it drops links whose
target is outside the known universe, because a link to nothing is dead
weight. In the application that is no longer true — a link towards a missing
card is now a branch to generate. Deezer's four `similar` are therefore kept
as they are; the MusicBrainz relations, however, stay reserved for existing
cards, otherwise any session musician would become a direction.

**Generation needs the network**, and 0016 wants the application to say so
rather than fail.

## To settle

- **Recomputing the vectors after a link is edited in session** — `aL`
  changes the text of both cards; their vectors only move at the next
  `forkstify vectors`. An edit only counts for the engine at the next launch
  anyway (the key table): the day the in-memory catalog follows edits, the
  vector will follow by the same path as generation (`embed::embed` in the
  background, `write_vector`).

- **One card, or its neighborhood?** Brel generated alone had three links
  into the void; Brassens had to be generated behind him for the branch to
  lead anywhere. Should we generate in cascade (the artist and their missing
  similars), at the risk of four times the network and four cards nobody
  asked for? The "on arrival" trigger makes the question less pressing: the
  neighborhood gets generated as you go there.

- **The MusicBrainz name is not always Spotify's.** A generated card takes
  the MusicBrainz name ("La Ruda"), and resolving a track searches Spotify
  by `track:… artist:<name>` — but Spotify says "La Ruda Salska" (Joel,
  2026-09-09). The tops are then not found even though the card carries the
  artist's Spotify identifier. Fixed by hand in the card; to settle: an
  alias field, or a resolution that checks the results' Spotify identifier
  rather than the name, or taking the Spotify name at generation time when
  the identifier is known.

- **Contributing back upstream.** [catalog.md](catalog.md) § Pooling wants a
  generated card that is missing from the reference to be proposed as a
  pre-chewed PR. Nothing does that; to be designed along with `:mine`.
  Designed on 2026-09-19 in [before-release.md](before-release.md)
  (workstream B, `cp`); the same workbook proposes `fg<n>` to generate a gap
  without taking it (workstream C).
